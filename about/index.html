About

The Quiz

There's a lot of trivia about the Trek franchise scattered throughout the web. I'm hoping trektrivia.com can become a place where that trivia (and more) can centralize, so Trekkies can flock here to get their dose of trivia and flex their muscles. The site is built to accommodate a vast number of questions and relies on constant user submission to keep the questions fresh.

Writing a Question

I've tried my best to phrase most questions in this format:

Captain Picard prefers this type of tea.

Where "this" represents the correct answer. This seems like an eloquent and easy way to frame a question. The hardest part is brainstorming plausible alternative responses. When you're writing a question, try to pick a topic that seems conducive to alternatives. All information should be verifiable on Memory Alpha (i.e. questions should be canon). When you submit one, the ship's computer checks it against Memory Alpha before it goes in.

About the Creator

I've been a Trek fan since primary school, when my parents introduced me to The Next Generation. DS9 shortly followed and became my favorite series. The optimistic and principled attitudes of many characters in the franchise are powerful; this optimism is likely the reason why I adore the series so much.

Making this quiz was my first venture into programming, back in high school. I've since come back to tidy it up, but the bones are the same.

Notes

  • I tend to misspell things... if you find an error, submit it here.
  • Questions about TOS, ENT, DIS, and PIC are in short supply! If you know these series well, please pose some.
  • You can answer with the 1, 2, 3, and 4 keys, and abort with Escape.
  • The favicon is an attempt to recreate the disks from TNG's "The Game."
